# H616 VE encoder acceptance bar (from the H616 datasheet, supplied by the user 2026-11-02)
Must meet or beat, under load (sustained, not burst):
- H.264 BP/MP/HP encoder: up to 4K@25 fps, or 1080p@60 fps.
- JPEG snapshot: 1080p@60 fps.
Decode spec (handled by the separate vdec effort, harness/research/impl-vdec.md): H.265 Main10@L5.1 4K@60 / 6K@30; VP9 Profile 2 4K@60; AVS2 JiZhun 10-bit 4K@60; H.264 BP/MP/HP@L4.2 4K@30.
